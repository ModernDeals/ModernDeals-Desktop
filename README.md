# ModernDeals Desktop

Point of sale and stock management for one shop, on one Windows computer, **with no internet
needed**.

## What it does

- **Point of sale:** sell with a barcode scanner or by searching, take cash, card or Instapay,
  give discounts, and print receipts.
- **Products and stock:** sizes and colours, stock received from suppliers, stock corrections,
  and low-stock alerts.
- **Returns and refunds,** with the stock put back automatically.
- **Expenses and profit:** record what the shop spends and see profit and loss for any period.
- **Customers:** keep a record of who bought what.
- **Barcode labels:** print labels on a label printer, in one click with no print dialog.
- **Staff:** separate accounts and permissions for the owner and staff.
- **Arabic and English,** switchable at any time.
- **Backups every day,** with a second copy to a USB drive or another folder, and restore in a
  few clicks.

Everything is stored on your computer. The app keeps working when the internet is down.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
- About 1 GB of free disk space
- Internet only for: activating a license (optional, it can be done without), a quick check-in
  at least once every 6 months, downloading updates, and moving your shop online

## Getting started

1. **[Download the installer](https://modern.deals/download/desktop)** and run it.
2. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the setup: your shop's name, your owner account, and where to keep backup copies.
4. Start selling. You have a **free 2-month trial** with every feature.

## License

Buy a license from [modern.deals](https://modern.deals) and activate it from the app's
**License** page, online in a few seconds, or without internet using the code shown on that
page. A license is for one computer and does not expire.

When the trial ends without a license, your records stay safe and readable: you can still look
at everything, print and back up, but not record new sales until you activate.

## Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version is available it downloads in the background while
you work, and installs the next time you open the app. A backup is taken before every update.
See [what's new in each version](CHANGELOG.md).

## Moving online

When your shop grows to more than one till, or you want an online store, you can move your
shop online. All your products, stock, sales, customers and staff move with you. Contact us at
[modern.deals](https://modern.deals) to upgrade.
